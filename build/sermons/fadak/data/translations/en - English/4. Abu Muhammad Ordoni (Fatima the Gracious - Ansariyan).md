# The Sermon of Fadak — Sheikh Abu Muhammad Ordoni
### From the Renowned Work: *Fatima the Gracious* (Ansariyan Publications, Qum)

> **Translator Attribution:**
> **Sheikh Abu Muhammad Ordoni** — Renowned translator and researcher for **Ansariyan Publications (مؤسسة أنصاريان للطباعة والنشر - قم المقدسة)**.
> **Publication:** Published in *Fatima the Gracious*, Chapter 6: "Abu Bakr Versus Fatima az-Zahra", extensively verified and distributed across Shia educational centers and Islamic platforms worldwide.

## Translation Text

### 1. Opening Praise, Divine Wisdom, and Monotheism (Tawhid)
“Praise be to Allah for that which He bestowed (upon us); And thanks be to Him for all that which He inspired; and commended in His Name for that which He Provided: From prevalent favors which He created, And abundant benefactions which He offered and perfect grants which He presented; (such benefactions) that their number is much too plentiful to compute; Bounties too vast to measure; Their limit was too distant to realize; He recommended to them (His creatures) to gain more (of His benefaction) by being grateful for their continuity; He ordained Himself praiseworthy by giving generously to His creatures;

I bear witness that there is no God but Allah Who is One without partner, a statement which sincere devotion is made to be its interpretation; hearts guarantee its continuation, and illuminated in the minds is its sensibility. He Who cannot be perceived with vision; neither be described with tongues; nor can imagination surround His state.

He originated the creation without a thing that was before it, and created them without emulating previous examples. He fashioned them with His power, and produced them with His will, without any need to their creation, nor any benefit for Him in their formation, except to establish His wisdom, and alert to His obedience, and manifest His power, and guide to His servitude, and grant dignity to His call. Then He assigned rewards to His obedience and placed punishment upon His disobedience, to safeguard His creatures from His wrath and direct them towards His Paradise.”

### 2. The Mission of the Prophet and the Bravery of Ali (as)
“And I bear witness that my father, Muhammad, is His servant and His messenger, whom He chose and designated before granting him the office of prophethood, and named him before sending him, and picked him before selecting him, when all creatures were concealed in the realm of the unseen, guarded under horrors, on the verge of non-existence.

Allah sent him to complete His decrees, execute His laws, and fulfill the measures of His mercy. He found the nations divided into multiple sects, devoted to their fires, worshiping their idols, and denying Allah despite their innate knowledge of Him.

Then Allah illuminated their darkness through my father Muhammad (s), removed the confusion from their hearts, and wiped the blindness from their eyes. He stood among the people for their guidance, delivered them from error, guided them to the firm religion, and called them to the Straight Path.

You were then on the brink of a pit of fire, a sip for the drinker, an opportunity for the greedy, a firebrand for the hasty, a footstep for the passersby, drinking dirty water, eating leaves from trees, humiliated and abased, fearing that men might kidnap you from around you!

Then Allah saved you through my father, Muhammad (s), after all the trials and tribulations, and after he was challenged by the fierce warriors of Arabia and the wolves of the People of the Book. Whenever they ignited the fire of war, Allah extinguished it; whenever the horn of Satan emerged, or an idolater opened his jaws, he would thrust his brother [Ali] into its fiery depths, who would not return until he had trampled their foreheads and quenched their flames with his sword, exhausting himself for Allah’s sake, striving in Allah’s cause, close to Allah’s Messenger, a master among Allah's friends, ever earnest, sincere, working hard, exerting himself, while you lived in luxury, secure and rejoicing, awaiting catastrophes to befall us, watching for news, and retreating from the battlefields!”

### 3. The Re-emergence of Hypocrisy and Usurpation of Fadak
“Yet, when Allah chose for His Prophet the dwelling of His prophets and the abode of His sincere servants, the thorns of hypocrisy emerged in you, the garment of religion became worn out, the chieftain of deviation spoke out, the obscure gained prominence, and the braying of falsehood arose and shook the grounds of your courtyards!

And Satan poked his head from his hole, calling upon you, and finding you ready to respond to his invitation, and observing you to be quick to receive his deceit!

Do you claim that I have no share, and that I do not inherit from my father?! Is there a ruling of the Days of Ignorance you seek?! And who is better in judgment than Allah for a people who have firm faith?!

O Ibn Abi Quhafa! Is it in the Book of Allah that you inherit your father, while I do not inherit my father?! Indeed you have brought a slanderous thing!

Did you intentionally abandon the Book of Allah and cast it behind your backs, when it explicitly says: *‘And Sulaiman inherited Dawood’* [27:16], and when it relates the prayer of Zakariya: *‘So grant me from Yourself an heir, who will inherit me and inherit from the family of Ya’qub’* [19:5-6], and He said: *‘And those who are related by blood are nearer to one another in the Book of Allah’* [8:75], and He said: *‘Allah commands you concerning your children: for the male, what is equal to the share of two females’* [4:11]?!

Did Allah single you out with a verse from which He excluded my father?! Or do you say: ‘The followers of two different faiths do not inherit from each other’?! Are my father and I not of one and the same faith?! Or are you more knowledgeable of the specific and general verses of the Quran than my father and my cousin?!

Here it is! Take it, all packed and saddled! It will meet you on the Day of Gathering, where the Judge is Allah, the leader is Muhammad, and the appointed time is the Resurrection! At the Hour, the falsifiers shall lose!”
