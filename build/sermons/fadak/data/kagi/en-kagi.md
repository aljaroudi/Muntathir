# The Sermon of Fadak — Complete Arabic Text
### By the Lady of the Women of the Worlds, Fatimah al-Zahra (peace be upon her)

> Delivered in the Prophet’s Mosque in Medina after the death of the Messenger of God (may God bless him and his family), and the seizure of Fadak and the caliphate.
> Principal sources: **Al-Ihtijaj by al-Tabarsi (vol. 1, pp. 131–149)**, **Bihar al-Anwar by Allamah al-Majlisi (vol. 29, pp. 215–335)**, **Kashf al-Ghummah by al-Irbili**, **Dala’il al-Imamah by al-Tabari the Shi‘i**, **Balaghat al-Nisa’ by Ibn Tayfur**.

---

## Introduction to the Account (How al-Zahra, Peace Be Upon Her, Went to the Mosque)

Abdullah ibn al-Hasan related, through his chain of transmission from his forefathers (peace be upon them): When Abu Bakr and Umar resolved to deny Fatimah (peace be upon her) Fadak, and word of this reached her, she wrapped her headscarf around her head and put on her cloak. Accompanied by a group of her attendants and the women of her people, she went forth, her skirts trailing beneath her feet. Her gait differed in no way from that of the Messenger of God (may God bless him and his family). She entered where Abu Bakr sat among a gathering of the Emigrants, the Helpers, and others. A curtain was hung between her and them, and she sat down. Then she sighed so deeply that the people broke into sobs and the gathering shook. She waited a little until their sobbing subsided and their agitation calmed. She began by praising and extolling God and invoking blessings upon the Messenger of God (may God bless him and his family), and the people wept again. When they fell silent, she resumed and said (peace be upon her):

## Text of the Blessed Sermon

IN THE NAME OF GOD, THE MOST MERCIFUL, THE MOST COMPASSIONATE

Praise be to God for the blessings He has bestowed, and thanks be to Him for the inspiration He has granted;

and praise for what He has given before: the boundless blessings He first bestowed,

the abundant favors He conferred, and the full measure of gifts He granted.

Their number is too great to count, their reach too vast to repay,

and their duration beyond comprehension. He called upon them to seek more through gratitude, that His blessings might continue;

He sought the praise of His creatures through His generosity and called them again to seek blessings like them.

And I bear witness that there is no god but God alone, without partner: a declaration whose meaning He made sincerity,

whose bond He placed within hearts, and whose meaning He illumined through reflection. Eyes cannot behold Him,

tongues cannot describe Him, and imaginations cannot conceive His nature.

He brought things into being from nothing that preceded them, and created them without following any model.

He formed them by His power and brought them forth by His will, not because He needed to create them,

nor because He stood to gain from shaping them,

but to establish His wisdom, call attention to obedience to Him, manifest His power, summon His creatures to worship,

and strengthen His call. Then He set reward for obedience to Him and punishment for disobedience,

to keep His servants from His wrath and draw them toward His Paradise.

And I bear witness that my father Muhammad is His servant and Messenger. He chose him before sending him,

named him before selecting him, and singled him out before commissioning him, when all creatures were hidden in the unseen,

sheltered behind the veil of the unknown, and bound to the uttermost limit of nonexistence. God Most High knew how matters would unfold,

encompassed the events of the ages, and knew where all things would come to pass.

God sent him to complete His command, carry out His decree,

and fulfill the measures of His mercy. He saw the nations divided in their religions,

devoted to their fires, worshipping their idols, and denying God despite knowing of Him.

Through my father Muhammad (may God bless him and his family), God lit up their darkness, lifted the obscurity from their hearts,

and cleared the veils from their eyes. He stood among the people to guide them, rescued them from error,

opened their eyes to their blindness, guided them to the upright religion, and called them to the straight path.

Then God took him unto Himself, in mercy and by choice, favoring him with what was better. Muhammad (may God bless him and his family) now rests from the toil of this world,

surrounded by the righteous angels, the favor of the ever-forgiving Lord, and the nearness of the Almighty King.

May God bless my father, His Prophet, His trusted one, His chosen one and beloved among His creatures; and may peace, God’s mercy, and His blessings be upon him.

Then she turned to those assembled and said:

You, servants of God, are the bearers of His commands and prohibitions, the carriers of His religion and revelation, and God’s trustees over yourselves,

and His messengers to the nations. He has a rightful claim upon you, a covenant He entrusted to you, and a legacy He left among you:

the speaking Book of God, the truthful Qur’an, the radiant light and shining illumination.

Its insights are plain, its inner meanings disclosed, and its outward meanings clear. Its followers rejoice in it;

following it leads to God’s pleasure, and heeding it leads to salvation. Through it one attains God’s illuminating proofs,

His explained ordinances, His prohibitions against forbidden things, His manifest signs, His sufficient arguments,

His commended virtues, His granted concessions, and His prescribed laws.

God made faith a purification for you from idolatry, and prayer a cleansing from pride;

almsgiving a purification of the soul and an increase in sustenance, and fasting a strengthening of sincerity;

pilgrimage a support for religion, and justice a means of bringing hearts into harmony;

obedience to us a foundation for the community’s order, and our leadership a safeguard against division;

struggle a source of honor for Islam, and patience an aid in earning reward;

enjoining what is right a benefit to all, and kindness to parents a protection against wrath;

maintaining family ties a means of lengthening life and increasing descendants, and just retribution a means of preserving lives;

fulfilling vows a way of seeking forgiveness, and giving full measure and weight a means of preventing fraud.

He forbade drinking wine as a cleansing from defilement, and false accusation as a protection from damnation;

and theft, so that people’s property might be secure. God forbade associating partners with Him so that devotion to His lordship would be sincere.

So fear God as He ought to be feared, and do not die except as Muslims.

Obey God in what He has commanded you and forbidden you, for among His servants, it is those with knowledge who truly fear God.

Then she said:

People! Know that I am Fatimah and that my father is Muhammad. I say this at the outset and say it again.

What I say is no mistake, nor are my actions excessive.

A Messenger has come to you from among yourselves; your suffering weighs heavily upon him; he is deeply concerned for you, and toward the believers he is compassionate and merciful.

If you trace his lineage and know who he is, you will find that he is my father, not the father of your women,

and the brother of my cousin, not of your men. What a noble bond it is to him—may God bless him and his family.

He delivered the message, proclaiming its warnings and turning away from the path of the idolaters.

He struck at their very center, seized them by the throat, and called to the way of his Lord with wisdom and good counsel.

He shattered idols and struck down their champions until their forces were routed and turned their backs.

Then night gave way to morning, and truth shone forth in its purity.

The leader of the faith spoke, the devils’ clamor fell silent, the rabble of hypocrisy collapsed,

and the bonds of unbelief and discord came undone.

You uttered the declaration of sincere faith alongside a band whose faces shone and whose bellies were lean.

You had stood on the brink of a pit of fire: a sip for the thirsty,

prey for the greedy, an ember snatched by a passerby, and ground trampled underfoot. You drank stagnant water

and lived on strips of dried hide, humiliated and cast aside, afraid that the people around you would snatch you away.

Then God, blessed and exalted, saved you through Muhammad (may God bless him and his family), after many trials,

and after he had faced formidable men, the wolves of Arabia, and the rebellious among the People of the Book.

Whenever they kindled a fire of war, God extinguished it. Whenever Satan’s horn arose,

or the idolaters opened their jaws, he sent his brother into their midst. He would not withdraw until he had crushed their power beneath his feet

and quenched their flames with his sword: worn down in God’s cause, striving in God’s command, close to the Messenger of God,

a leader among God’s friends, resolute, sincere, diligent, and tireless, fearing no one’s reproach in God’s cause.

Meanwhile, you lived in comfort, at ease, enjoying yourselves in safety.

You waited for calamity to befall us, watched for news, drew back when battle began, and fled the fighting.

But when God chose for His Prophet the abode of His prophets and the resting place of His chosen ones,

the thorn of hypocrisy appeared among you; the cloak of religion grew threadbare; those who had stifled their misguidance spoke out; the obscure among the lowly rose to prominence;

and the champion of falsehood roared and strutted through your midst. Satan raised his head from his hiding place and called to you.

He found you ready to answer, eager to be deceived. He urged you on and found you quick to move;

he provoked you and found you angry. So you branded camels that were not yours and led them to a watering place not yours.

And all this while the covenant was still fresh, the wound still wide and unhealed,

and the Messenger not yet buried. You claimed you had rushed to act for fear of discord.

But behold: into discord they have fallen, and Hell surely encompasses the unbelievers.

How far you have strayed! What has come over you? How can you be turned away when the Book of God is among you, its teachings plain,

its rulings radiant, its signs brilliant, its warnings manifest, and its commands clear?

Yet you have cast it behind your backs. Do you wish to turn away from it, or judge by something other than it?

What a wretched substitute for the unjust! Whoever seeks a religion other than Islam, it will never be accepted from them, and in the Hereafter they will be among the losers.

You did not even wait for its restlessness to subside or its reins to grow easy to hold before you began to stoke its fire

and stir its embers, answering the call of the wayward Satan, extinguishing the lights of the manifest religion,

and neglecting the practices of the chosen Prophet. You secretly pursued your designs beneath a show of something else, and moved against his family and children under cover and in secret.

And we endure from you what feels like knives cutting into us and spearpoints piercing our innermost being.

Now you claim that we have no inheritance. Do you seek the judgment of the Age of Ignorance?

And who is better than God in judgment for a people who are certain? Do you not know?

Indeed, it is as plain to you as the midday sun that I am his daughter.

Muslims! Am I to be deprived of my inheritance?

Son of Abu Quhafah! Does the Book of God say that you inherit from your father, but I do not inherit from mine?

You have brought forth a monstrous thing. Have you deliberately abandoned the Book of God and cast it behind your backs when it says, “Solomon inherited from David,”

and recounts Zechariah’s plea: “Grant me, from Yourself, an heir who will inherit from me and inherit from the House of Jacob”?

And it says, “Blood relatives are nearer to one another in the Book of God,”

and, “God instructs you concerning your children: for the male, a share equal to that of two females,”

and, “If one leaves wealth, a bequest should be made for parents and close relatives in fairness—a duty upon the God-fearing.”

Yet you claim that I have no share, that I do not inherit from my father, and that there is no bond of kinship between us. Has God singled you out with a verse that excludes my father?

Or do you say that people of two different faiths do not inherit from one another?

Are my father and I not of the same faith?

Or do you know the Qur’an’s specific and general provisions better than my father and my cousin?

Then take it, bridled and saddled; it will meet you on the Day of your gathering.

God is the finest judge, Muhammad will speak for us, and the appointed time is the Resurrection. At that Hour, those who uphold falsehood will lose.

Your regret then will do you no good. Every prophecy has its appointed fulfillment,

and you will come to know who will be struck by a humiliating punishment and upon whom an enduring punishment will fall.

Then she turned her gaze toward the Helpers and said:

O assembly of leaders, pillars of the community, and guardians of Islam! Why this disregard for my right, this indifference to the wrong done to me?

Did not my father, the Messenger of God (may God bless him and his family), say, “A person is honored through the care shown to his children”?

How quickly you have changed! How swiftly this has happened, though you have the ability to support what I seek and the strength to secure what I ask.

Do you say that Muhammad has died?

It is a grievous calamity whose breach has widened, whose rupture has spread, and whose seams have torn apart.

The earth has darkened at his absence;

the sun and moon have been eclipsed, and the stars scattered by the calamity of his loss.

Hopes have withered, mountains have bowed, what was sacred has been neglected, and sanctity has been cast aside at his death.

By God, that is the greatest disaster and the most tremendous affliction. None like it has ever descended,

nor has any sudden calamity like it been proclaimed. The Book of God, exalted be His praise, announced it in your courtyards,

morning and evening. Its recitation rang out among you—called aloud, proclaimed, chanted, and sung.

It is what befell God’s prophets and messengers before him: a final judgment and an inescapable decree:

“Muhammad is only a messenger; messengers have passed away before him. If he dies or is killed, will you turn back on your heels? Whoever turns back on his heels will not harm God in the least, and God will reward those who are grateful.”

Children of Qaylah! Is my father’s inheritance to be taken from me while you see and hear me, gathered here together?

My appeal reaches you, and you know what has happened. You have numbers, provisions, equipment, and strength; you have weapons and armor.

My call reaches you, but you do not answer. My cry comes to you, but you offer no help. Yet you are renowned for striving,

known for goodness and righteousness—the chosen people selected to stand with us, the People of the House.

You fought the Arabs, bore hardship and toil, confronted nations, and faced formidable foes. We never ceased to lead you, and you followed our commands,

until the mill of Islam turned in our favor, the days yielded their bounty, the clamor of idolatry was subdued, and the surge of falsehood fell still;

the fires of unbelief died out, the call to chaos was silenced, and the order of religion stood firm. Why, then, did you turn aside after truth had been made clear,

hide yourselves after declaring yourselves openly, retreat after advancing, and associate others with God after believing?

Woe to those who broke their oaths after pledging them, plotted to drive out the Messenger, and attacked you first.

Do you fear them? God is more worthy of your fear, if you are believers.

But I see that you have settled into comfort and pushed aside the one most entitled to govern.

You have withdrawn into ease and fled from the breadth of your duty into the confines of complacency. You have spat out what you once accepted

and cast up what you once swallowed. If you and everyone on earth disbelieve, God is surely self-sufficient, worthy of praise.

Know that I have spoken as I have, fully aware of the abandonment that has taken hold of you

and the treachery lodged in your hearts. Yet my words are an outpouring of grief, a release of anger, a cry born of anguish, a disclosure of what weighs upon my breast, and the presentation of my case.

O daughter of the Messenger of God! Your father was tender, generous, compassionate, and merciful to the believers,

and a painful torment and severe punishment to the unbelievers.

If we speak of his lineage, we find that he is your father above all other women,

and the brother of your beloved above all other companions.

He preferred him to every dear friend and supported him in every grave undertaking.

None but the fortunate love you, and none but the wretched and estranged hate you.

You are the pure family of the Messenger of God, the chosen and elect.

You are our guides to goodness and our paths to Paradise.

And you, chosen among women and daughter of the best of prophets,

are truthful in what you say and foremost in the soundness of your judgment.

Your right is not denied, nor is your truth rejected.

By God, I have not departed from the judgment of the Messenger of God, nor acted without his permission; a guide does not lie to his people.

I call God to witness—and God suffices as a witness—

that I heard the Messenger of God say:

“We, the company of prophets, leave neither gold nor silver, neither houses nor landed property as inheritance. We leave only the Book, wisdom, knowledge, and prophethood. As for anything provided for our use, the one in authority after us may dispose of it according to his judgment.”

We have devoted what you seek to mounts and weapons

with which Muslims fight, strive against the unbelievers,

and battle the wicked rebels. This was done by the consensus of the Muslims.

I did not decide it alone, nor impose my opinion by myself.

As for my own circumstances and wealth, they are before you and at your disposal; they will not be withheld from you or kept for anyone else.

You are the lady of your father’s community and the goodly tree from which your children spring. Your merit will not be denied,

nor will your lineage, root or branch, be diminished. Your word carries weight over all that is in my hands.

Do you truly think I would oppose your father (may God bless him and his family) in this?

She said: Glory be to God! My father, the Messenger of God, did not turn away from the Book of God or contradict its rulings.

Rather, he followed its guidance and observed its precepts.

Will you add to your betrayal by falsely claiming his authority for it?

What you do after his death resembles the plots laid against him during his life. Here is the Book of God, just in its judgment and decisive in its words.

It says, “He will inherit from me and inherit from the House of Jacob,” and it says, “Solomon inherited from David.”

God, mighty and majestic, has made clear the shares He apportioned, the obligations and rules of inheritance He prescribed,

and the portions He allowed for men and women. In doing so, He swept away the excuses of those who uphold falsehood and removed doubt and uncertainty for later generations.

No! You have let your own desires persuade you of something. So patience is most fitting, and God is the One whose help is sought against what you claim.

Abu Bakr said: God has spoken the truth, His Messenger has spoken the truth, and his daughter has spoken the truth. You are a wellspring of wisdom, a home of guidance and mercy, a pillar of religion, and a source of proof. I do not dismiss your judgment or deny your words.

These Muslims stand between you and me. They entrusted me with the office I hold, and it was by their agreement that I took what I took—not through arrogance, despotism, or a desire to claim it for myself. They are witnesses to that.

Fatimah (peace be upon her) then turned to the women and said: Muslims, so quick to speak falsehood and so ready to overlook a vile and ruinous deed!

Will you not ponder the Qur’an, or are there locks upon your hearts?

No! Your evil deeds have covered your hearts with rust.

They have overtaken your hearing and your sight. How wretchedly you have interpreted the matter, how badly you have counseled, and how terrible the bargain you have made!

By God, you will find its burden heavy and its consequences dire when the veil is lifted and the harm behind it becomes clear,

when your Lord shows you what you never expected. Then those who upheld falsehood will be lost.

## After the Sermon: At the Grave of the Messenger of God (May God Bless Him and His Family)

Then she (peace be upon her) turned toward the Prophet’s grave (may God bless him and his family) and said:

Since you departed, strange events and turmoil have come to pass ... Had you witnessed them, there would have been no need for so many words.
We have lost you as the earth loses its abundant rain ... Your people have fallen into disorder; witness them, and do not be absent.
Would that death had found us before you ... Before word came of your death and the grave shut you away from us.
Men turned grimly upon us and treated us with contempt ... Since you left us, they have seized every good thing.
The one who took charge will learn on Judgment Day ... How he wronged our protector when I came seeking my right.
Men revealed what their hearts held against us ... When we lost you, and they seized the whole inheritance.
Every people have kin and standing ... Before God, and those closest to them have the strongest claim.
We have suffered a loss such as none among creation ... Whether Arab or non-Arab, has had to bear.

### Abu Bakr’s Exchange with al-Zahra (Peace Be Upon Her) and Her Reply

**Abu Bakr’s reply:**
Abu Bakr answered her, saying:
“O daughter of the Messenger of God, your father was tender, generous, compassionate, and merciful to the believers, and a painful torment and severe punishment to the unbelievers. If we speak of his lineage, we find that he is your father above all other women, and the brother of your cousin above all other men. Your cousin preferred him to every dear friend and supported him in every grave undertaking. None but the fortunate love you, and none but the wretched hate you. You are the pure family of the Messenger of God (may God bless him and his family), the chosen and elect, our guides to goodness and our paths to Paradise. And you, chosen among women and daughter of the best of prophets, are truthful in what you say and foremost in the soundness of your judgment. Your right is not denied, nor is your truth rejected. By God, I have not departed from the judgment of the Messenger of God or acted without his permission. A guide does not lie to his people. I call God to witness—and God suffices as a witness—that I heard the Messenger of God say: ‘We, the company of prophets, leave neither gold nor silver, neither houses nor landed property as inheritance. We leave only the Book, wisdom, knowledge, and prophethood. As for anything provided for our use, the one in authority after us may dispose of it according to his judgment.’ We have devoted what you seek to mounts and weapons with which Muslims fight, strive against the unbelievers, and battle the wicked rebels. This was done by the consensus of the Muslims. I did not decide it alone or impose my opinion by myself. My own circumstances and wealth are before you and at your disposal; they will not be withheld from you or kept for anyone else. You are the lady of your father’s community and the goodly tree from which your children spring. Your merit will not be denied, nor will your lineage, root or branch, be diminished. Your word carries weight over all that is in my hands. Do you think I would oppose your father in this?”

**The reply of Lady Fatimah al-Zahra (peace be upon her):**
She (peace be upon her) said:
“Glory be to God! The Messenger of God (may God bless him and his family) did not turn away from the Book of God or contradict its rulings. Rather, he followed its guidance and observed its precepts. Will you add to your betrayal by falsely claiming his authority for it?! What you do after his death resembles the plots laid against him during his life!

“Here is the Book of God, just in its judgment and decisive in its words. It says, ‘HE WILL INHERIT FROM ME AND INHERIT FROM THE HOUSE OF JACOB,’ and it says, ‘SOLOMON INHERITED FROM DAVID.’ God, mighty and majestic, has made clear the shares He apportioned, the obligations and rules of inheritance He prescribed, and the portions He allowed for men and women. In doing so, He swept away the excuses of those who uphold falsehood and removed doubt and uncertainty for later generations. No! ‘YOU HAVE LET YOUR OWN DESIRES PERSUADE YOU OF SOMETHING. SO PATIENCE IS MOST FITTING, AND GOD IS THE ONE WHOSE HELP IS SOUGHT AGAINST WHAT YOU CLAIM.’”

### Her Exchange with the Commander of the Faithful, Ali ibn Abi Talib (Peace Be Upon Him), Upon Returning Home

Then she (peace be upon her) returned. The Commander of the Faithful (peace be upon him) had been awaiting her return and watching for her arrival. Once she had settled at home, she said to him:
“Son of Abu Talib, you have wrapped yourself up like an unborn child and sat apart like one under suspicion! You broke the falcon’s wing, and its feathers failed you when you had no weapon left! This son of Abu Quhafah is robbing me of my father’s gift and the means of supporting my two sons! He confronted me openly in our dispute, and I found him fiercely obstinate in his words. The children of Qaylah withheld their aid from me, the Emigrants their support, and the whole assembly looked away. No one defended me or stood in his way. I went out holding back my anger and returned humiliated. You bowed your cheek when you let your sword lose its edge! You once fought wolves, but now you lie upon the dust! You stopped no one from speaking and achieved nothing of consequence. I have no choice. Would that I had died before seeing this day and suffering this humiliation! May God judge between me and you—between you who wronged me and you who should have defended me! Woe to me at every sunrise; woe to me at every sunset! My pillar has died and my support has weakened. To my father I bring my complaint, and to my Lord I bring my grievance. O God, You are greater in might and power, and more formidable in strength and punishment.”

The Commander of the Faithful (peace be upon him) replied:
“Woe is not yours; woe is your enemy’s. Restrain your grief, daughter of the Chosen One and remnant of prophethood. I have not neglected my faith or failed to do what lies within my power. If it is provision you seek, your sustenance is assured and the One who cares for you is trustworthy. What has been prepared for you is better than what has been taken from you. Seek your reward from God.”

She (peace be upon her) said, “God is sufficient for me,” and fell silent.
