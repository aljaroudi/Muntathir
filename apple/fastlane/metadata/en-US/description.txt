Prayer and daily remembrance, together in Muntathir.

PRAYER AND REMEMBRANCE
See local prayer times, choose a calculation method, and set reminders. Follow the changing sky on Today, read a daily Quran verse, and share it as a card. Find Qibla with map and alignment guidance. Count with interactive 3D tasbih beads, use front-camera assistance to track rakaat, and review your prayer, reading, and remembrance activity.

READ AND REFLECT
Read the Quran verse by verse or in Mushaf pages, choose translations, and return to your reading position. Choose which Hadith books to keep for offline browsing and search. Explore supplications and rulings, bookmark passages, highlight selected text, add notes, and share text or image cards. Personal highlights and notes sync through your private iCloud account when available.

SEARCH YOUR WAY
Search downloaded Quran and Hadith text offline. Try online meaning search when local text search finds no results; your selected book and chapter stay in scope.

DATES AND PEOPLE
Browse Islamic observances and explore figures and family relationships. Choose the system calendar, local naked-eye sighting for the Middle East, or Saudi government announcements. Adjust individual months by up to two days. Dates follow calculated Umm al-Qura values until matching confirmations are available.

ACROSS YOUR DEVICES
Made for iPhone and iPad, with an Apple Watch companion and Home Screen widgets. Available in 15 interface languages, including Arabic and English.

Arabic Quran text and English translation are bundled. Other translations and library content require downloads before offline reading. Online meaning search requires an internet connection. Prayer times and calendar dates depend on your settings; follow your local authority where appropriate.
